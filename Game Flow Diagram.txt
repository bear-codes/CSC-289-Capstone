# Blackjack Game Flow Diagram

## 1. Overview

This document describes the overall flow of a Blackjack round from lobby creation through round completion. It defines the order of operations, turn transitions, and decision points used by the program.

---

## 2. Game Flow Steps

1. **Lobby Creation & Join**

   * Players join a lobby (1–6 players) before the game starts.

2. **Game Start & Deck Shuffle**

   * Game is initiated, deck is created and shuffled, player and dealer states are reset.

3. **Initial Deal**

   * Each player receives two face-up cards, dealer receives one face-up and one face-down card.

4. **Player Turns**

   * Players act sequentially in join order.
   * Actions include Hit, Stand, Double Down, and Split.

5. **Split Hand Handling**

   * If a player splits, each hand is played independently before moving to the next player.

6. **Dealer Turn**

   * Dealer reveals hidden card and hits until hand total is 17 or higher.

7. **Hand Evaluation**

   * Each player hand is compared to the dealer to determine Win, Loss, or Push.

8. **Results & Round End**

   * Display outcomes for all hands.
   * Players may start a new round or leave the lobby.
