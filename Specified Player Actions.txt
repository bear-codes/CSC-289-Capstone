Specified Player Actions



## 1. Overview

Player actions define the choices a player may make during their turn in a game of Blackjack. Actions are governed by the current state of the game, the player’s hand, and specific rule constraints.

Each action results in a state change that affects gameplay flow.

---

## 2. Hit

**Description:**

* The player requests an additional card from the deck.

**Availability:**

* Allowed when the hand value is less than 21
* Allowed on any non-completed hand (including split hands, except split Aces)

**Effect:**

* One card is added to the current hand
* Hand value is recalculated

**End Conditions:**

* Player may continue to Hit if hand value remains under 21
* Hand ends immediately if the hand value exceeds 21 (bust)

---

## 3. Stand

**Description:**

* The player chooses to take no more cards and end the current hand

**Availability:**

* Allowed at any time during the player’s turn

**Effect:**

* The current hand is marked as complete

**End Conditions:**

* Control passes to the next hand (if split) or next player

---

## 4. Double Down

**Description:**

* The player doubles their bet in exchange for receiving exactly one additional card

**Availability:**

* Allowed only on the player’s initial two-card hand
* Not allowed on split hands

**Effect:**

* One card is dealt to the hand
* Bet multiplier for the hand is set to 2×

**End Conditions:**

* Player automatically stands after receiving the card

---

## 5. Split

**Description:**

* The player divides their initial two-card hand into two separate hands

**Availability:**

* Allowed only when the first two cards are of the same rank
* Allowed only once per player per round

**Effect:**

* Original hand is replaced with two hands
* Each new hand receives one additional card

**End Conditions:**

* Player must complete the first split hand before playing the second

---

## 6. Split Aces (Special Case)

**Description:**

* A special restriction applies when splitting Aces

**Availability:**

* Occurs only if the player splits two Aces

**Effect:**

* Each Ace hand receives exactly one additional card

**Restrictions:**

* No additional hits allowed
* No doubling allowed

**End Conditions:**

* Both hands automatically stand after receiving one card

---

## 7. Invalid Actions

The following actions are not permitted and should be blocked by the program:

* Hitting after standing
* Doubling down after receiving more than two cards
* Splitting hands that are not the same rank
* Performing any action after a hand has busted

---

## 8. Action Resolution Order

When an action is selected:

1. Validate action against current hand state
2. Apply rule constraints
3. Update hand and game state
4. Check for end-of-hand conditions
5. Pass control to the appropriate next hand or player

---

## 9. Action State Flags (Recommended)

To manage actions cleanly, each hand should track:

* `isActive`
* `isCompleted`
* `isBusted`
* `isDoubled`
* `isSplitHand`

---

## 10. Scope Notes

* No surrender or ins
