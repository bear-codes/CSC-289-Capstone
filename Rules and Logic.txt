# Blackjack Program Rules

## 1. Game Overview

* Game type: Blackjack (21)
* Number of players: 1–6 human players
* Dealer: Automated (computer-controlled)
* Platform: Desktop application
* Players join a single lobby/room per game session

---

## 2. Deck Rules

* The game uses **one standard 52-card deck**
* The deck is shuffled at the start of each game
* Card values:

  * Cards 2–10: face value
  * Jack, Queen, King: value of 10
  * Ace: value of 1 or 11 (automatically chosen to produce the best hand not exceeding 21)

---

## 3. Initial Deal

* Each player is dealt **two cards face up**
* The dealer is dealt:

  * One card face up
  * One card face down (hidden)

---

## 4. Player Turn Actions

During their turn, a player may choose one of the following actions:

* **Hit**: Receive one additional card
* **Stand**: End their turn
* **Double Down**: Double the bet and receive exactly one additional card, then stand
* **Split**: If the first two cards are the same rank, split them into two separate hands

---

## 5. Doubling Down Rules

* Doubling down is allowed only on the player’s initial two-card hand
* The player receives exactly **one additional card**
* The player automatically stands after receiving the card
* Bet value is logically doubled (no real-money or chip system required)

---

## 6. Splitting Rules

* A split is allowed only when the player’s first two cards are of the same rank
* When splitting:

  * The hand is divided into two separate hands
  * Each new hand receives one additional card
* Each split hand is played independently
* A player may split **only once** (maximum of two hands)
* Special rules for Aces:

  * If Aces are split, each hand receives only one additional card
  * No further hits are allowed on split Ace hands

---

## 7. Turn Order with Split Hands

* If a player splits, they must complete their first hand before playing the second hand
* Other players wait until all split hands are finished before the turn moves on

---

## 8. Blackjack Rules

* A Blackjack is defined as a hand totaling 21 using exactly two cards
* Blackjacks cannot occur on split hands
* Player Blackjack:

  * The player’s turn ends immediately
* Dealer Blackjack:

  * The dealer reveals the hidden card immediately
  * All non-Blackjack player hands lose

---

## 9. Dealer Rules

* The dealer reveals the hidden card after all players have completed their turns
* Dealer must:

  * Hit until the hand total is **17 or higher**
  * Stand on 17 or higher
* The dealer does not split or double down

---

## 10. Win and Loss Conditions (Per Hand)

Each player hand is evaluated individually against the dealer:

* Player hand busts (over 21): Loss
* Dealer busts and player hand is 21 or less: Win
* Player hand total greater than dealer total: Win
* Player hand total less than dealer total: Loss
